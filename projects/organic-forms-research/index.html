Organic Forms

Visual research

An ongoing visual research on nature and organic forms. I began working with plant forms after realising that nature, for me, is connected to friction. It stands against the smoothness of designed systems and offers something slower, less controlled and more meaningful. My idea of nature is closely tied to my grandparents’ vineyards, where I photographed leaves and observed patterns of growth, seasonal changes and recurring structures. These images became the starting point for my visual research.

I am interested in how natural growth and digital repetition can exist alongside each other, and how organic forms can interrupt these patterns. I look at structures such as the veins of leaves and their similarities to the looping logic of digital systems.

Through photography and screen printing, I explore repetition, movement and small changes in form. The research is still developing and is continuously informing my graphic design practice.
