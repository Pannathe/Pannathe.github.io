Flower Language

Visual communication system

Flower Language is a system of symbols designed to communicate both positive and negative messages through floral forms. It creates a visual language based on beauty, mysticism and sensation rather than direct instruction. The project brings together the clarity of street signs and the ornamental, symbolic qualities of flowers.

Inspired by the formal structures of Pictograms, Icons & Signs by Rayan Abdullah and Roger Hübner, the project takes the familiar language of signage in a more personal and expressive direction.
