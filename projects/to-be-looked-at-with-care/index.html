To Be Looked At With Care

Short film

A short film exploring the idea that femininity should be just for the self. Not a standard to meet or a choice between power and softness, but a form of self-expression. It challenges the idea that being feminine means being weak, or that independence is not part of femininity.

Through quiet, poetic scenes set in nature and domestic spaces, the film explores femininity as a personal, almost private something.
